Micropaleontology

Module: Consolidation of Basics II (PB-V2)
Microfossils are the most numerouse fossil organisms. In this course, students learn about the basics of this useful group of fossils in a systematic, group-by-group fashion.

Lead instructor

Bastian Seidl

Instructors

Bastian Seidl

Form

1.5 class every week.

Credits

3 ECTS

Type

Regular course

Evaluation

Written exam at the end of the semester with the biostratigraphy course.
